*Welcome!*

I'm Andrew. This is my little digital world on the internet—a place where I grow my thoughts, however transient or matured. I'm an MD/PhD student at Boston University, and my days are defined by precision.

This garden is its antithesis: an intentional, low-entropy corner for quiet, incomplete, even fragmented thinking. It exists as a space for observations, budding thoughts, and half-formed models can coexist without competing for bandwidth or colliding into noise. 

When I’m not in the lab or the hospital, I am usually looking north toward the mountains. I spend an unreasonable amount of cognitive RAM thinking about high altitudes, cold granite, and alpine ridgelines, moving through terrain where gravity is binary, feedback is instantaneous, and the internal monologue is forced into absolute silence. I still haven't fully figured out why they've colonized my subconscious, but I've stopped arguing with the draw.

Consider this an open working bench.
